I've never really read much after leaving primary school, but I have started to read more recently.

Books I'm reading right now:

  • Shadow Slave- This is a webnovel by Guiltythree I started around December last year when I was abroad in Saudi Arabia.
  • A Game Of Thrones (ASOIAF)- Yes, this is the Game of Thrones show, I'm reading the book, written by George R R Martin, and I honestly think it's better than the show.
  • Red Rising- Written by Pierce Brown I've just started this book and I already really like it, I like the protagonists character and his relationship with his wife. It's leading up to become quite intense, so I'm excited to see where it goes.

Books I am planning to read:

  • The Wheel Of Time- A-14 book epic fantasy series by Robert Jordan, about a young farm boy who learns he is the reincarnation of an ancient saviour to fight a supreme evil
  • The Stormlight Archive- An ongoing epic fantasy series by Brandon Sanderson set on 'Roshar', a harsh planet battered by powerful, continent-wide storms.
  • Malazan- Written by Steven Erikson and Ian Cameron Esslemont, Malazan is a massive epic fantasy series about an expanding human empire, warring Gods, and the heavy cost of suffering.

As you can probably tell I enjoy fantasy and sci-fi books the most, those are the types of stories I tend to enjoy and find interesting, especially when theres a lot of background lore and world building. I'm not very good at explaining the books- but they are a lot more interesting than I've described.
